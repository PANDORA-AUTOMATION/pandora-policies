Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This policy explains how the PANDORA Applications Discord bot handles information used for Roblox verification and application management.

1. Information processed

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Roblox user IDs, usernames, and display names.
  • Roblox group membership and role information.
  • Application answers and application status.
  • Reviewer IDs, notes, denial reasons, and timestamps.

2. Roblox verification

Roblox OAuth is used to verify the Roblox account connected to a Discord user. OAuth access tokens are used during authentication and are not intended to be kept as permanent user records.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to verify Roblox accounts, confirm PANDORA group membership, determine application eligibility, process and review applications, update Roblox group roles after approval, and help prevent duplicate or abusive submissions.

4. Access

Application information may be visible to authorized PANDORA staff who review or administer applications. Discord and Roblox also process information required for their respective services to function.

5. Data sale

PANDORA does not sell information collected through the bot.

6. Storage and retention

Information may be stored for as long as needed for verification, application history, personnel management, moderation, security, and abuse prevention.

7. Removal requests

Users may request removal of information associated with their Discord or Roblox account by contacting PANDORA administration through the official PANDORA Discord community.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the bot or its data practices change. The latest version will be available on this page.

9. Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to PANDORA administration through the official PANDORA Discord community.
